# Promo video

Source for the Meloday Music promo video (1920x1080, 60 fps, about 77 s). Everything is generated by code:
the footage is real screenshots of the app, the music is synthesized, nothing is sampled or borrowed.

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `music.py` | Synthesizes the 112 BPM music bed (drums, bass, pads, plucks, lead), 34 bars plus a tail. |
| `engine.py` | A small compositing engine: easing (the app's own curve), Nunito text, perspective-tilted windows with soft shadows, the brand wave, film grain. |
| `promo.py` | The fourteen scenes and the cut (on the music's bar lines), then the render and mux. |

## Making it

Inputs live in `artifacts/promo/` (not committed: they contain the album art of a personal library):

* `shots2/`: 1920x1080 screenshots of the app window (`home_2`, `home_3`, `mfy`, `mfy_3`, `radio`, `radio_2`, `radio_3`, `pulse`, `layers`, `artist`, `artist_2`, `trends`, `trends_2`, `trends_3`, `browse`). The Plex server name under "Music" in the sidebar is painted out when they're loaded.
* `radio/<name>/NNNN.jpg`: frames of the app's own radio artwork videos (`ffmpeg -i Assets/Radio/<name>.mp4 -vf scale=480:480 radio/<name>/%04d.jpg`).
* `assets/`: `MelodayMusic-symbol.png` (copied from the app's Assets).

```
python music.py ../../artifacts/promo/music.wav
python promo.py --stills 6 12 21 37        # single frames to artifacts/promo/stills, to check the layout
python promo.py --render ../../artifacts/promo/MelodayMusic-Promo.mp4
```

Needs Python 3 with numpy, scipy and Pillow, and ffmpeg on the path. A full render takes about 15 minutes.

## Notes

* The Meloday sky covers are redrawn from the app's own palette (`MelodaySun.cs`), so they match the app exactly.
* The AutoMix graphic is an illustration of a sixteen-bar blend (the in/out curves and the bass swap are the plan the app makes for a perfect fit); its songs and tempos are not real tracks.
* Cuts land on bar lines of the music (bar = 2.143 s): 0 intro, 2 title, 4 Home, 7 Made for You, 10 Meloday, 12 Live, 14 Pulse Guide, 16 Meloday Engine, 18 AutoMix (first drop), 22 artist page, 24 Trends, 26 mood playlists, 28 montage (second drop), 32 outro.
